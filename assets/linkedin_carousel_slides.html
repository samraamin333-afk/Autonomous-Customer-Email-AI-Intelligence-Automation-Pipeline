Stage 1: Ingestion & Data Hygiene

Taming Noisy Real-World Customer Inboxes

Raw customer emails are cluttered with nested tables, inline CSS, disclaimers, and endless reply threads.

🧼 1. BeautifulSoup HTML Sanitization

Removes DOM artifacts, scripts, and unescapes entities while preserving semantic paragraph structure.

✂️ 2. RFC 3676 Signature Removal

Heuristically strips '-- ' delimiters, signoffs ('Best regards'), mobile markers ('Sent from iPhone'), and corporate legal disclaimers.

🧵 3. Thread & Quote Isolation

Cuts historical Outlook/Gmail headers and '>' quote blocks to isolate solely the newest customer query.

Payload Noise Reduced by 16.6% Average 2 / 5
Stage 2: Hybrid AI & Multi-Factor Triage

Smart Categorization & Severity Scoring

🗂️ 8-Way Hybrid Classifier

Fuses Calibrated SGD TF-IDF probabilities with domain rule heuristics across:

• Tech Issue • Billing • Refund • Account • Order • Delivery • Cancellation • General

🚨 Multi-Factor Urgency Engine

Detects 4 severity tiers (Critical 1h SLA, High 4h SLA, Medium, Low) by fusing:

• Sev-1 Outages • Legal/GDPR Risks • $500+ Exposure • Exclamation cues

🔍 Structured Entity & Problem Extraction

Regex-guided entity parsing pulls Order IDs (#ORD-99120), Invoices (INV-88231), Tracking codes (TRK-8812903), and Error codes (ERR_SRV_500).

Enforcing Automated 1-Hour SLA Escalations 3 / 5
Stage 3: Sequence-to-Sequence Generation

Hallucination-Free T5-Small Responses

Instead of unconstrained generation, responses are strictly conditioned on verified corporate policies and extracted details.

respond support: category: Technical Issue | urgency: CRITICAL | policy: Critical outages route to L3 on-call | problem: API 500 error | action: Escalate immediately | details: ACC-94821, ERR_SRV_500

🧠 Fine-Tuned T5-Small

Trained with Hugging Face Seq2SeqTrainer for edge speed (<50ms) and zero token API costs.

🛡️ Business Policy Guardrails

Automatically inserts exact SLA commitments, return portals, and security protocols.

Accurate, Personalized, Compliant Responses 4 / 5
